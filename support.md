# Project S.O.S support

Project S.O.S is made by Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company.

## What the app does

Hold the red SOS button and the app sends your location, the emergency type and your details to the people you chose for that emergency. It sends them two ways at once: as a text message from your phone, and as an encrypted app message to people who also use the app.

The app does not call 911, 999, 112 or any other emergency service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

## Common questions

### Why do I have to tap Send on the text?

Phones do not let apps send texts on their own without special permission. So the app opens your messaging app with the text already written, and you tap Send. The encrypted app message goes without that tap, as long as you have internet.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. The app makes a private code for you on your phone. That code is how other people add you.

### How do I add someone?

Go to the People tab and add them with their phone number and their app code. To get their code, scan the QR code on their phone or open the invite link they send you. Then check safety words together. Each of you sees two lists of words. Read both lists aloud, in person or on a call. If they match, you know the code really belongs to that person.

### Do the people I add need the app?

No. Anyone with a phone number gets the text. People who also have the app get the encrypted app message too.

### What happens if I lose my phone?

Everything is stored on the phone, so Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company cannot restore it for you. To be ready, go to Settings, then Backup, and save a backup file somewhere safe, such as your computer or a cloud drive. The file is locked with a passphrase you choose. Keep the passphrase too. The company cannot recover it.

On a new phone, install the app and choose "Restore from a backup instead". If your backup includes your code, your people will still recognise you. If it does not, send them your new code so they can add you again.

Anyone with your backup file and its passphrase could send alerts as you. Keep both private.

### What are relays?

Relays are public servers run by other people. They pass on your encrypted app messages. Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company does not run them. They cannot read your messages, but they can see your IP address, when you send, and the app code of the person you send to. You can change the list of relays in Settings, then Relays. Texts do not use relays.

### Is my alert guaranteed to arrive?

No. The app is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number first. Texts need mobile signal. App messages need internet and at least one working relay. A flat battery can stop everything. Try a test alert with your people so you know how it works for you.

### Can the app record when I send an alert?

Yes, if you turn it on. It can use the phone's camera and microphone, GoPro cameras over Bluetooth (early support), Bluetooth trigger buttons, and devices on your network that start from a web address. You can also stream live or upload to a service you set up. On iPhone, phone video stops when the app goes into the background, and recording carries on with sound only.

### Can I record without sending an alert?

Yes. "Record sound only" on the SOS screen records sound and tells nobody. Dashcam mode (Settings, Recording, Dashcam mode) records on a loop and keeps only the last few minutes. If you press SOS while it is on, those minutes are kept with the alert. Both stay encrypted on your phone. On Android a notification shows while they record. On iPhone, video stops while the app is in the background, and sound carries on.

### Is it legal to record?

That depends on where you are. Some places require everyone being recorded to agree. You are responsible for following the law where you are. If you are not sure, check before you turn recording on. This includes sound recordings without an alert, dashcam mode and "Answer calls during an alert", which lets the caller hear what is near your phone.

### Can I change how the app looks?

Yes. Go to Settings, then Appearance, and pick one of 28 themes, or build your own. There are themes for night use, bright sun, high contrast, colour-blindness and greys only. The app also works with large text, screen readers and reduced motion.

### What data do you collect?

The app sends Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company nothing. The company has no servers, no analytics and no ads. If you email the company, it keeps your email to answer you. Read the full privacy policy: https://elderguardadmin-byte.github.io/project_s.o.s_privacy_policy/.

### How do I delete my profile and data?

There is no account on any server, so everything is on your phone. To delete it:

1. Open Project S.O.S.
2. If an alert or a check-in timer is running, end it first.
3. Go to Settings, then Data, then Delete my profile.
4. Read the notes, then hold the button until it fills. You can also tap to delete instead, then confirm.

The app deletes your username, your app code, your people, history, recordings, notes, trips and settings from this phone, and starts again as if newly installed. This cannot be undone. Messages you already sent stay on your people's phones. Backups you saved yourself are not touched. Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the phone.

More detail: https://elderguardadmin-byte.github.io/project_s.o.s_privacy_policy/delete-your-data

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company cannot issue them itself. Your legal rights as a consumer still apply. See section 7 of the terms of use.

- App Store: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
- Google Play: go to play.google.com, open Account, then Order history, and choose Request a refund. Within 48 hours of buying, a refund is usually quick.

In the UK and EU you may have 14 days to cancel. Apple lets you ask through reportaproblem.apple.com in that time.
