# Delete your profile and data

App: **Project S.O.S**, by Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company.

Project S.O.S has no accounts on any server. Your profile (your display name and your app code, which is a key pair made on your phone) and everything else the app stores live only on your own device. Upton Software Ltd. Liability Company never receives them, so there is nothing for the company to delete on its side.

## Delete your profile in the app

1. Open Project S.O.S.
2. If an alert is on, end it first.
3. Go to **Settings**, then **Delete my profile**.
4. Read what will be deleted, then confirm.

The app then deletes, from this phone:

- your display name and your app code (the keys in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore)
- your people, groups and emergency types
- your alert history, recordings and their keys, notes and trips
- your check-in timer, reminders and settings

The app then starts again as if it were newly installed. This cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored on the device.

## What is not deleted

- **Messages already delivered.** Alerts and updates you sent stay on your people's phones. A public relay may keep its copy of an encrypted message for a while; it cannot read it.
- **Your own backups.** Copies in backup storage you set up yourself (WebDAV or S3), and backup or export files you saved, are not touched. Delete them there.
- **Your old app code on other phones.** People who added you keep your old code until they remove you. Tell them if you start again with a new one.
- **Support emails.** If you emailed the company, it keeps those emails for up to two years. To have them deleted sooner, email [elderguardadmin@elderguard.info](mailto:elderguardadmin@elderguard.info).
